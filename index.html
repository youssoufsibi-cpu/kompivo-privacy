KOMPIVO Privacy Policy

Developer: YS Innovation
App: KOMPIVO
Package: com.kompivo.app
Effective date: October 26, 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how KOMPIVO handles information when you use the KOMPIVO Android app. KOMPIVO is an on-device video compressor. You do not need a KOMPIVO account. YS Innovation does not operate a KOMPIVO backend that stores or processes your videos.

2. Video and media processing

When you select a video using Android’s video picker, KOMPIVO reads the selected video and relevant media information, such as its display name, file size, duration, dimensions, and video and audio tracks, to prepare and perform compression. Compression runs locally on your Android device. KOMPIVO does not upload selected source videos or compressed videos to a YS Innovation server. KOMPIVO does not modify the original source video.

Some local operation and recovery records may contain the selected video’s Android content URI and display name so KOMPIVO can restore an in-progress or recoverable operation. These records are not sent to a YS Innovation server.

3. Information handled by KOMPIVO

KOMPIVO stores information on your device needed to provide its features, including:

  • Compressed videos saved in KOMPIVO’s private library.
  • Library and History details, which may include a source or output display name, completion date and time, original and compressed sizes, and references to the compressed result.
  • Local operation, Batch, and recovery records. Depending on the operation, these may include selected-video URI references, display names, and progress or result state.
  • Gallery-save status needed to check a copy you explicitly saved.

Filenames and media metadata can sometimes contain personal information. These details remain in KOMPIVO’s local app storage unless you choose to share a video, save a copy to Gallery, or otherwise make it available outside the app as described below.

4. Google Play Billing and other services

KOMPIVO uses Google Play Billing for its one-time Premium purchase. Google Play handles the purchase flow and payment processing. The app receives product and purchase information needed to show the Google Play price, recognize or restore the purchase, and acknowledge it with Google Play. KOMPIVO does not send purchase tokens to a YS Innovation server. KOMPIVO does not ask for or receive your full payment-card or bank credentials through the app.

Google may process account, transaction, device, and technical information under its own terms and privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy. YS Innovation does not control Google’s data handling.

When you use Android’s Open or Share actions, the compressed video is provided to the app you choose. When you explicitly choose Save to Gallery, KOMPIVO creates a copy in your device’s shared Movies/KOMPIVO location. Android, Gallery, or another app may then access that copy under its own rules. KOMPIVO does not control how those services or apps handle information.

This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical visit information, including IP addresses, for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Storage and retention

KOMPIVO keeps its private compressed videos and related Library records on your device until you delete them or remove KOMPIVO’s app data. KOMPIVO does not automatically upload these videos or scan your device to import other videos.

KOMPIVO retains records for private Library videos while those videos remain in the Library. Older non-private History references are limited to 100 entries; when that limit is exceeded, older metadata may be removed. Removing metadata does not itself delete a public Gallery copy. KOMPIVO does not use age-based automatic deletion for private Library videos.

Copies you explicitly save to Gallery are stored outside KOMPIVO’s private library. They remain until you delete them using Gallery, a file manager, or another suitable Android feature. Deleting a KOMPIVO private copy does not delete a separately saved Gallery copy, and deleting a Gallery copy does not delete the private copy.

KOMPIVO configures Android backup and data-transfer exclusions for its app-private data. Some Android or device-manufacturer transfer processes may operate differently, so YS Innovation cannot guarantee that every device-transfer mechanism will honor these exclusions.

6. Sharing

If you choose Open or Share, Android passes access to the selected compressed video to the viewer or sharing app you choose. That app may store, process, or transmit the video according to its own practices. Review the recipient app’s privacy information before sharing. KOMPIVO does not receive a copy of the video back through this action unless you separately select it in KOMPIVO.

7. Permissions

KOMPIVO uses Android’s video picker so you select which source videos it can access; it does not request broad video-reading or all-files access. The app uses foreground-service and, where applicable, notification capabilities to run and report compression. On older Android versions, it includes a legacy write permission limited to Android 9/API 28 and below for compatible Gallery saving. Google Play Billing requires network communication with Google Play.

8. Security

KOMPIVO stores its private videos and local records within Android’s app-private storage and configures backup exclusions. KOMPIVO does not add separate encryption to video files; their protection depends on Android’s app sandbox and the security settings of your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free.

9. Children’s privacy

KOMPIVO is a general-purpose video utility and does not ask users to create an account or provide personal details such as a name, age, or contact information. YS Innovation does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through a KOMPIVO account or backend service. Google Play handles any purchase flow under its own policies and safeguards.

If you have a concern about information related to a child, contact YS Innovation using the contact details below.

10. Changes to this policy

YS Innovation may update this policy if KOMPIVO’s features or data handling change. The revised policy will identify its updated effective date and be made available at the public policy address used for KOMPIVO. Material changes should also be reflected in KOMPIVO’s Google Play disclosures.
